# A grace day when computing the fine

Readers complain about being fined for being one day late. We change **only the way the fine
is computed**, nothing else.

- The first day past the due date is free: fine = (number of days past the due date − 1) × rate,
  no less than `0.0`. A return one day past the due date → fine `0.0`; two days past the due
  date of a book → `1.0`.
- A loan one day past its due date **is still overdue**: it blocks new loans
  (`has_overdue`), it is in the overdue report with `days_overdue: 1` and it gets the
  `past_due` notification. The grace applies to money, not to the due date.
- Fines already charged before this change stay unchanged.
